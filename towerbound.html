Ongoing Project — Early Prototype

3D Action Tower Defense

An early prototype of a 3D action tower defense where players fight alongside their defenses, possess towers for high-impact plays, and combo attacks with structures to repel escalating enemy waves. Core systems are in place and actively being expanded.

Combat & Movement ✓

Player movement and core combat are up and running. The player can reposition freely, dodge incoming attacks, and engage enemies with a working weapon set. Attack variety — light, heavy, and ranged options — is currently being expanded and refined.

GOAP AI System ✓

Both enemies and towers run on a Goal-Oriented Action Planning (GOAP) system — an AI architecture where agents dynamically plan sequences of actions to reach a goal, rather than following fixed scripts. Currently implemented with foundational behaviours, with more complex actions planned as the prototype develops.

Tower Possession Planned

Players will be able to step into any tower and take direct control, gaining boosted stats and a powerful ability at the cost of leaving their character exposed. Knowing when to possess and when to stay mobile will be a core part of high-level play.

Combat–Tower Combo System Planned

Player attacks and tower attacks will amplify each other through a shared debuff and combo system. Hitting a tower-marked enemy — or baiting one into a tower's fire — will trigger bonus damage and visual feedback, rewarding coordinated play between the player and their defenses.

Technical Highlights

GOAP AI Architecture

Goal-Oriented Action Planning lets both enemies and towers decide their own behaviour at runtime by evaluating a set of goals and planning the cheapest sequence of actions to achieve them. Unlike behaviour trees or state machines, GOAP agents can adapt to changing conditions without needing manually authored transitions — making it easier to add new actions and scale complexity as the prototype grows.

Player Movement & Combat

The player controller handles 3D movement, dodge rolling, and weapon switching. Weapons are fully implemented with their own stats and behaviours. Attack logic — including light, heavy, and ranged variants — is currently being built out, with hit detection and animation integration in progress.

Tower Possession System

Planned feature. When implemented, possession will transfer input control from the player character to a selected tower, switching it from autonomous GOAP-driven firing to direct player aim. A stat multiplier and unique ability will activate on entry, with the player character left vulnerable until possession ends.

Combat–Tower Combo System

Planned feature. A shared status system will track debuffs from both the player and towers. Hitting a debuffed enemy with the other source triggers a combo multiplier, applying bonus damage and a visual effect. Each tower type will define its own debuff and combo behaviour, making the system extensible as new towers are added.
